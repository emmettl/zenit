What makes “one plane”?

The featured group is an orientation neighbourhood measured from propagated angular momentum at the opening UTC. It is not an operator-assigned plane, service status or availability claim.

Thin circles illustrate orientation at the sampled radius. They are diagrams, not observed tracks, coverage footprints or links between satellites. Point sizes and colours are authored. Earth hides objects on its far side.

A bounded source

Positions are calculated directly with satellite.js 7.1.0, SGP4 WGS72/AFSPC, using TEME elements and a GMST transform into the Earth-fixed view. Every visible record shares the displayed UTC. Absolute element offset must remain within 24 hours; eligibility can change during playback. This is a dated geometric study.

CelesTrak standard GP. Upstream: USSPACECOM / 18th Space Defense Squadron via Space-Track.org. Basic GP fields only; group membership and names are provider-supplied. No claim of complete operational membership, communications service or optical visibility.
